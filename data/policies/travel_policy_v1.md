# Northstar Travel Cover — Sample Policy v1

> Fictional training document. Not an insurance contract.

## Trip cancellation

The sample plan reimburses eligible prepaid trip costs up to 5,000 currency units when a covered reason listed in the policy prevents travel. A claim must include the booking receipt, cancellation confirmation, and documents supporting the covered reason. A change of plans is not, by itself, a covered reason.

## Emergency medical expenses

The sample plan reimburses eligible emergency medical expenses abroad up to 50,000 currency units. The traveler or a representative should contact the assistance line as soon as practical for hospitalization or a major procedure. The policy does not replace local emergency services.

## Exclusions

Expenses arising from travel against a doctor's written advice, routine checkups, and losses caused by an undeclared pre-existing condition are excluded from this sample plan. Coverage depends on the policy definitions and claim review.

## Claims process

Notify Northstar within 30 days after returning home. Submit the policy number, itemized receipts, proof of payment, and any medical or carrier reports relevant to the claim.

## Definitions

- **Covered reason:** a reason explicitly listed as eligible under the applicable policy section.
- **Pre-existing condition:** a condition that existed before the policy's coverage start date, as defined in this sample policy.
