# Northstar Auto Cover — Sample Policy v1

> Fictional training document. Not an insurance contract.

## Collision cover

The sample plan covers accidental damage to the insured vehicle after the policyholder pays a collision deductible of 500 currency units per covered event. The maximum payment for vehicle damage is the lower of the approved repair cost or the vehicle's stated insured value of 20,000 currency units. The policyholder must report an accident within 48 hours and provide the incident date, location, and available photographs.

## Comprehensive cover

The sample plan covers theft and fire damage after a comprehensive deductible of 250 currency units per covered event. Theft must be reported to local authorities. A claim review may request the police report and proof of ownership. The stated insured value is a limit, not a guaranteed payout.

## Exclusions

The sample plan does not cover damage caused while the vehicle is used for paid passenger transport, intentional damage caused by the policyholder, or ordinary wear and tear. A claim is not approved solely because an event is reported; the policy terms and documentation are reviewed.

## Claims process

1. Notify Northstar within 48 hours of the incident.
2. Provide the policy number, incident details, and supporting documents requested by the claims team.
3. Wait for the review and repair authorization before treating an estimate as approved.

## Definitions

- **Deductible:** the amount the policyholder pays toward a covered event before the plan pays its covered share.
- **Stated insured value:** the maximum value used for this sample policy's vehicle-damage limit.
