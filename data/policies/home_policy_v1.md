# Northstar Home Cover — Sample Policy v1

> Fictional training document. Not an insurance contract.

## Building cover

The sample plan covers direct accidental damage to the insured building from fire, lightning, and windstorm, subject to a 1,000 currency-unit deductible per event and a maximum building limit of 250,000 currency units. The policyholder should take reasonable steps to prevent further damage and keep receipts for emergency repairs.

## Contents cover

The sample plan covers household contents up to a total limit of 40,000 currency units. A single item is limited to 2,000 currency units unless it is listed separately on the policy schedule. Proof of ownership or value may be requested during claim review.

## Exclusions

Flood damage, gradual water seepage, and damage caused by lack of maintenance are excluded from this sample plan. A separate endorsement would be required for flood cover; none is included in this document.

## Claims process

Report property damage within 72 hours after it is discovered. Include the policy number, date discovered, a description of the damage, and photographs if available. The claims team may request an inspection before authorizing permanent repairs.

## Definitions

- **Building limit:** the maximum amount available for covered building damage under this sample policy.
- **Endorsement:** a written change that adds to or modifies policy terms.
